    Walrath Solutions

    Trucking, specialty materials, and landscape supply — one family company out of Tacoma, Washington.

    Started in 1968 by Tom Sr. as an owner/operator, then joined in 1987 by Tom Jr., who now runs the company with his own family. Walrath operates with over 120 employees at the main branch, three bulk material yards, three nursery locations, and a specialty soil blending facility — serving the greater Puget Sound, including Tacoma, Gig Harbor, Olympia, and the greater Seattle area.
